How the lanes and the order are read

A project that is in-game, at stage 6, is also read on three lanes. Each step is this page’s reading of what the project reports about itself, or of the source its row names, and an empty meter means no figure is reported. Blue marks the steps that meet the playable bar: 20 frames a second and 30 minutes without a crash.

Rows at that stage are ordered by their lanes and nothing else: fewest steps short of the bar on speed and stability together, then the better picture, the higher frame rate and the longer session. The lanes were suggested in issue 8 on this page’s repository.

Picture

What the project says is on screen in the game's world.

  1. 1Mostly dark or unlit
  2. 2Lit where the sun falls, shadows black or dark
  3. 3Lit in light and shade, with visible faults
  4. 4Lit and whole, small faults only
  5. 5The console's picture, ray tracing included

Speed

The middle of the frame rates it reports in play, in rendered frames a second (fps).

  1. 1Under 2 fps
  2. 22 fps or more
  3. 35 fps or more
  4. 410 fps or more
  5. 520 fps or more
  6. 630 fps or more, the console's rate

Stability

The longest session it reports without a crash.

  1. 1Under 10 minutes
  2. 210 minutes or more
  3. 330 minutes or more
  4. 4An hour or more

Method and limits

  • Playable builds

    None exist. Any download that claims to be GTA VI for PC is fake, and fake builds carrying malware have circulated since August.

  • The build

    These projects work from a leaked Xbox build. Rockstar has not released or announced a PC version. This page does not link to the leak or explain how to get or run it.

  • Sources

    Each project's public repository and open pull requests and, where marked, its chat. Project names link to those repositories to credit the work.

  • Stage claims

    Every stage is the project's own claim unless its source line says otherwise, and most are unverified. CGBJ's comes from press reports, because its code is private.

  • Frame rates

    Rendered frames. Frames added by frame generation are reported as such and do not count toward the playable bar.

  • Lanes

    A project at stage 6 is also read on three lanes: picture, speed and stability. Each step is this page's reading of what the project reports about itself, speed is the middle of the frame rates it reports, and the key under the standings title lists the steps.

  • Tested on

    The systems and graphics card makers a project or its testers report running it on. It may run on others, and a row with none listed has reported none.

  • Crash reports

    By one project's notes, the game's crash reporter sends data to an outside server each time it crashes.

  • Who compiles it

    An AI assistant (Claude), automatically, about every 15 minutes. It can be wrong.

  • Affiliation

    None with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Microsoft or any project listed.

  • Positions

    Rows at stage 6 are ordered by their lanes alone: fewest steps short of the playable bar on speed and stability together, then the better picture, the higher frame rate and the longer session. Lower stages are ordered by this page's reading of what remains before the next stage. A place is computed from what each project reports about itself: it is not a verification or an endorsement. An equals sign means level.
